MISSION TITAN — NEW ESP32 38-PIN USB-C CP2102 WIRING

ESP32 board in the supplied photo: ESP32-WROOM-32 / 38-pin style board.

SAFE FIRST TEST
- New ESP32 alone over USB data cable.
- Do not connect battery, BMS, INA219 or DS18B20 until Blink upload succeeds.

SENSOR WIRING
1) INA219
- VCC -> ESP32 3V3
- GND -> ESP32 GND
- SDA -> GPIO21
- SCL -> GPIO22
- INA219 VIN+ / VIN- are the current-measurement path; do NOT put the battery negative current path through GND.
- For pack/load current, use the intended high-side path and verify the exact BMS/load topology before powering it.

2) DS18B20 x2 (shared 1-Wire bus)
- Sensor VDD -> ESP32 3V3
- Sensor GND -> ESP32 GND
- Both DATA pins -> GPIO4
- One 4.7k resistor from GPIO4/DATA bus -> 3V3

3) JBD DP04S007 UART
- JBD UART pin 1 GND -> ESP32 GND
- JBD UART pin 2 RXD -> ESP32 GPIO17 (TX)
- JBD UART pin 3 TXD -> ESP32 GPIO16 (RX)
- JBD UART pin 4 VDD/B+ -> DO NOT CONNECT until the exact board/version and required UART logic/power are verified.

4) JBD DP04S007 balance connector J1
- BC0 -> pack negative / Cell 1 negative
- BC1 -> Cell 1 positive
- BC2 -> Cell 2 positive
- BC3 -> Cell 3 positive
- BC4 -> Cell 4 positive

5) JBD power terminals
- B- -> battery pack negative
- Pack positive -> battery/load/charger positive as specified by the BMS wiring diagram
- C- -> common negative for charger/load on DP04S007 versions that use C- for charge/discharge negative

IMPORTANT
- Do not rely on wire colour alone for the balance harness. Verify pin order with the board/model documentation and a meter before connecting the pack.
- Do not connect the JBD balance connector to ESP32 GPIOs.
- Do not connect JBD VDD/B+ to ESP32 3V3/5V blindly.
